🥃 Whiskey Advent

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Whiskey Advent is a private, invitation-only app for a group of friends to rate a whiskey a day through December. This page explains what it stores about you and why.

What we collect

  • Your account: when you sign in with Google, your name, email address and profile photo. If you sign up with email instead, your name, email address and the password you choose (stored securely by our login provider; we never see it).
  • Your photo: if you upload a profile picture, it's cropped and shrunk on your device first, and only that smaller copy is stored.
  • What you add: your ratings, tasting notes, flavor tags, comments and reactions, and the time you rated each day (used for streaks and awards).
  • Your settings: tasting mode, theme, notification preferences and your time zone (so the daily reminder arrives at 5 pm where you are).
  • Invitations: if an admin invites you, the name and email address or phone number they entered, so your account can be matched to the invitation when you join. The invitation itself is sent from the admin's own phone or email, not by us.
  • Notifications: if you turn on reminders, a notification address for your device, issued by Apple or Google.
  • Usage analytics: Google Analytics records which screens are used and basic device and browser information, using cookies. It is linked to an internal account number, not your name or email.

The app also keeps a few things in your browser's storage: your sign-in, your theme, and a pending invite link until you've used it.

How it's used

Only to run the app: showing your ratings and the group's results, working out streaks and awards, sending the emails and notifications you've asked for, and understanding which features get used. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Other members of the group can see your name, photo, ratings, tasting notes, flavor tags, comments and any awards you win. Nothing is shown to anyone outside the group. The app's admins can also see account details, such as your email address, in order to approve and manage members.

Photos (whiskey photos and uploaded profile pictures) are stored at web addresses that aren't listed or searchable, but anyone who has a photo's exact address can open it.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database, photos and login) and the app is served by Cloudflare. Sign-up and password emails are sent through Resend. Notifications are delivered through Apple's and Google's push services, and analytics are processed by Google.

How long it's kept

Your ratings and notes are kept from year to year so the group can look back at past seasons. If you sit a year out, your history stays and is linked back up when you return.

Your choices

You can change your photo, tasting mode and settings, and turn notifications off, at any time in your profile. To get a copy of your data, or to delete your account and everything you've added, ask the admin who invited you.

Age

The app is only for people of legal drinking age and isn't meant for children. If we learn an account belongs to someone underage, it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
