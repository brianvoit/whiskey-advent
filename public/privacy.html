🥃 Whiskey Advent

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Whiskey Advent is a private, invitation-only app for a group of friends to rate a whiskey a day through December. This page explains what it stores about you and why.

What we collect

  • Your account: when you sign in with Google, your name, email address and profile photo. If you sign up with email instead, your email address and the password you choose (stored securely by our login provider; we never see it).
  • What you add: your ratings, tasting notes, flavor tags, comments and reactions.
  • Your settings: tasting mode, theme, notification preferences and your time zone (so the daily reminder arrives at 5 pm where you are).
  • Notifications: if you turn on reminders, a notification address for your device, issued by Apple or Google.
  • Usage analytics: Google Analytics records which screens are used and basic device and browser information. It is linked to an internal account number, not your name or email.

How it's used

Only to run the app: showing your ratings and the group's results, sending the notifications you've turned on, and understanding which features get used. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Other members of the group can see your name, photo, ratings, tasting notes, flavor tags and comments. Nothing is visible to anyone outside the group. The app's admins can see account details in order to approve and manage members.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and login) and the app is served by Cloudflare. Notifications are delivered through Apple's and Google's push services, and analytics are processed by Google.

Deleting your data

You can turn notifications off at any time in your profile. To delete your account and everything you've added, ask the admin who invited you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
